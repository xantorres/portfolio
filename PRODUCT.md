# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, founders, and hiring managers evaluating a senior remote candidate. First contact is a 20 to 60 second triage pass, usually one tab among thirty, on a laptop in daylight. The second visit is a deeper technical read, often by an engineer or CTO deciding whether the seniority claim holds. Both audiences arrive skeptical: they have seen hundreds of template portfolios and AI-generated landing pages this year.

## Product Purpose

Convert a visit into contact for senior remote roles: Senior Frontend Engineer, Product Engineer, AI-native Full-Stack Builder, Agentic Software Engineer, Product Architect. Success is the visitor thinking "we need to talk to this person before someone else does" and acting on it. Secondary success is shareability: the site is distinctive enough to be forwarded with the words "look at this one."

## Brand Personality

Precise, kinetic, assured. The site behaves like a calibrated instrument built by its owner: every surface demonstrates the engineering it claims. Emotional sequence for the visitor: quiet awe on arrival ("how was this made?"), growing trust through proof density ("this person documents like a senior engineer"), mild urgency at the close ("available now, will not stay available").

## Anti-references

- The editorial-typographic lane the site previously occupied: mono labels, ruled separators, rule-separated columns, monochromatic restraint. Saturated to death; reads as AI-default in 2026.
- Generic SaaS landing composition: centered hero, icon-card grids, gradient CTAs.
- AI-slop signifiers: purple gradients, glassmorphism, decorative orbs, gradient text.
- Dev-portfolio category reflexes: near-black plus safe blue, terminal cosplay, fake Matrix rain.
- Awwwards-clone gimmickry: cursor trails, scroll-jacking, effects that demonstrate nothing.

## Design Principles

1. The site is the first case study. Every surface must demonstrate the engineering it claims: real diagrams from real systems, motion with the polish of shipped product UI.
2. Proof outranks polish. Real numbers, real architecture, real tools. Decoration exists only when it carries evidence.
3. Choreograph attention. One dominant idea per fold. Motion exists to guide the 30-second recruiter scan from claim to proof to contact.
4. Instrument, not toy. Every interactive detail must feel calibrated and purposeful. If an effect does not inform, it goes.
5. Commit, do not hedge. Color, type, and layout decisions are carried through at full strength. Timid averages read as invisible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Axe gate stays: no serious violations on any route (enforced by Playwright + axe test suite).
- Full keyboard operability; visible focus states on the dark ground.
- prefers-reduced-motion gets a complete static experience, not a broken half-animation.
- Contrast at WCAG AA minimum everywhere, including text over the committed accent color.
